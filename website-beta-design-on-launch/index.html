How the endowment works

One campaign. A lifetime of giving. Then many more.

Most charities spend what they raise. We are doing something different: building a permanent base of capital whose earnings fund good work every year, while the original endowment stays fully intact.

  1. 1

    You give

    Every contribution is pooled into the endowment campaign and restricted solely to building the foundation's permanent capital.

  2. 2

    The principal is locked

    The $1 billion principal is permanently preserved. It is never spent, never drawn down, and never put at risk on operations.

  3. 3

    It is invested for the long term

    The endowment is prudently invested in a diversified portfolio built for steady, durable growth across decades.

  4. 4

    Returns are given back, every year

    Investment earnings fund grants and programs across our focus areas, year after year, for as long as the endowment stands.

Our focus

Solving problems here at home.

There is no shortage of need in our own neighborhoods, towns and cities. The Alec Davis Foundation is built to meet it, with the overwhelming majority of its giving directed to communities across the United States.

A permanent endowment means those communities can count on support that does not fade with the news cycle or the economy. It will be there next year, next decade, and next century.

  • American communities first. Local needs, local partners, lasting results.
  • Long-term thinking. Grants designed for durable change, not quick headlines.
  • Accountability. Clear reporting on where every grant goes and what it achieves.
