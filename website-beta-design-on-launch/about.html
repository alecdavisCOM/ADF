A foundation built for generations.

The Alec Davis Foundation exists to do good that lasts. We are building a permanent endowment so that our support for American communities never depends on the next fundraiser and never runs dry.

Who we are

Philanthropy designed to outlast us all.

The Alec Davis Foundation is a private foundation in its building stage. Founded on the belief that the most meaningful giving is the kind that never stops, it is being built around a single, permanent endowment.

Rather than spending what we raise, we preserve it. The endowment's principal is protected forever, and only the returns it generates are given away. That simple structure turns every contribution into a source of support that renews itself each year, for as long as the foundation stands.

When complete, the foundation will fund carefully selected programs across 13 pillars of impact, from protecting children and restoring trafficking survivors to accelerating medical research and supporting the men and women who keep our communities safe.

What we believe

The values behind every decision

Perpetuity

We measure success in generations, not fiscal years. Every structural decision protects the foundation's ability to give forever.

Stewardship

Every dollar entrusted to us is treated as a responsibility. Capital is managed with discipline, prudence and care.

Home first

We believe in solving problems at home. The overwhelming majority of our work will serve communities across the United States.

Compassion

Behind every program is a person: a survivor, a veteran, a child, a family. We never lose sight of the people we serve.

Transparency

Supporters deserve to know where their generosity goes. We commit to clear, honest reporting at every stage.

Measurable impact

We fund what works. Grants are evaluated on outcomes, so that each year's giving is wiser than the last.

Fiscal sponsorship

Our partnership with United Charitable

During the endowment campaign, the Alec Davis Foundation operates as a fiscally sponsored program of United Charitable, a registered 501(c)(3) public charity. The partnership is limited to one purpose: administering the foundation's Capital Reserve and Endowment Campaign.

All incoming contributions are held by United Charitable as restricted capital reserve assets, dedicated solely to the future capitalization and launch of the Alec Davis Foundation. No program distributions are required during this accumulation phase, and 100% of net contributions remain invested.

Once the campaign reaches its goal and the new entity receives IRS approval, the full endowment will be transferred by exit grant to the Alec Davis Foundation, a nonprofit private operating foundation.
