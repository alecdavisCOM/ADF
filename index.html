The vision

A permanent home for giving, at alecdavis.org

One day, alecdavis.org will be the home of a fully established private foundation, with an endowment that funds meaningful work year after year and a team that puts it to good use.

This page is the first step. It's a public promise that the foundation is coming, and an open invitation to the people who can help it arrive sooner.

What it will stand for

Built on three simple principles

Built to last

An endowed foundation meant to keep giving for decades, not a single campaign that ends when the money runs out.

Giving with purpose

Every dollar directed carefully toward people and causes where it can make a real, measurable difference.

Open and accountable

Founding partners will always know where their support goes and what it achieved. No surprises.

An honest note

The dream is ready. The funding isn't, yet.

I'll be straightforward: right now I don't have the resources to establish and endow a private foundation on my own. I'm not willing to let the idea wait until I do.

If you're a philanthropist or someone in a position to give, and this vision speaks to you, I'd be grateful to hear from you. A conversation is the best place to start.
