Legacy giving

Leave a legacy that gives forever.

A gift from your estate to a permanent endowment is never spent. It keeps generating support every year, so your generosity continues long after your lifetime, for your children's grandchildren and beyond.

Your assets can do good for centuries.

You can leave all of your estate, part of it, or a specific asset to support the foundation's endowment. Even a small percentage makes a lasting difference, and it costs you nothing today.

Charitable gifts at death are generally free of federal estate tax, and retirement accounts left to charity avoid the income tax your heirs would otherwise owe. That means more of what you built goes to the causes you care about.

A simple example

Leaving just 5% of a $500,000 estate adds

$0

to the permanent endowment, which could produce about $1,250 every year, forever, at an illustrative 5% payout.

Ways to leave a legacy gift

Will or living trust

Name the foundation for a specific amount, a percentage, or the remainder of your estate after loved ones are provided for.

IRA, 401(k) & retirement accounts

Name us as a full or partial beneficiary. Retirement assets pass to charity free of the income tax heirs would pay.

Life insurance

Designate the foundation as a beneficiary of a policy you own, in whole or in part.

Bank & brokerage accounts

Add a payable-on-death or transfer-on-death designation. Simple, private and easy to change.

Real estate & property

Leave a home, land or other property to be added to the endowment.

A percentage of anything

Any share counts. A small percentage of an estate can become a permanent source of support.

Sample bequest language

I give [a specific amount / ___ percent of my estate / the residue of my estate] to United Charitable, a 501(c)(3) public charity, for the benefit of its Alec Davis Foundation program, to be used for the endowment of the Alec Davis Foundation.

How to name the foundation today

While the endowment campaign is underway, the Alec Davis Foundation is a fiscally sponsored program of United Charitable, a registered 501(c)(3) public charity. For now, please list United Charitable as the beneficiary, designated for the Alec Davis Foundation program.

Once the foundation is formally established, you will be able to name the Alec Davis Foundation directly. Gifts made to the program will become part of the endowment transferred to the new foundation.

For the tax ID and exact designation details, email alec@alecdavis.com.
