Where your gift goes

Most donations are spent once. Yours will be protected, invested and put to work for good, every year, forever.

1. Restricted

Your contribution is held as restricted capital, dedicated solely to building and launching the Alec Davis Foundation endowment.

Before you give

Common questions

Will my gift be spent?

No. Your gift becomes part of the endowment's permanent principal. Only the investment returns the endowment generates will ever be given away, so your original gift keeps working indefinitely.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Gifts are received by a registered 501(c)(3) public charity serving as our fiscal sponsor, and are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. You will receive an acknowledgment for your records.

What happens to my gift during the campaign?

It is held as a restricted capital reserve asset, dedicated solely to launching the Alec Davis Foundation, and remains invested. No program distributions are required during this phase.

Can I change or cancel a monthly gift?

Of course. Monthly gifts can be changed, paused or cancelled at any time. Just contact us.
